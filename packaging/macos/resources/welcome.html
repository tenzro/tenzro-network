Install Tenzro

This installs the tenzro command line and the tenzro-node program, and checks that your Mac can run them.

  • Nothing is started and nothing joins the network.
  • No background service is installed.
  • Everything goes to /usr/local/lib/tenzro, with links in /usr/local/bin, and a launcher called Tenzro in Applications.
  • To remove it later: sudo tenzro-uninstall. Your keys and data are never touched.

The programs inside are the exact files of the signed Tenzro release (see the SHA-256 list at downloads.tenzro.com). This installer itself is not yet signed or notarized by Apple, which is why macOS asked you to confirm opening it.
