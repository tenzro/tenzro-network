App transport echo

A manual check of a node's app transport (preview) from a real browser: two devices in this page sign in to one node, the second echoes what the first sends, and the page reports the round trip. Nothing leaves the page except the WebTransport sessions to the node.

  1. On the node, configure [app_transport] and read tenzro_getAppTransportInfo: it gives the session URL and the SHA-256 hashes of the certificates the node serves. Check the publication's signing time and each hash's validity before you trust a hash (the SDK's verifyPublication does this).
  2. This page made two device keys. Issue each an access token of that node bound to its thumbprint (dpop_jkt), for two different identities.
  3. Paste the URL, one current hash and both tokens, then run.
